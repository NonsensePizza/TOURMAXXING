Central Mongolia offers a fascinating introduction to the country's history, landscapes, and cultural heritage. Here, monumental landmarks stand alongside rolling hills, rocky mountains, and traditional countryside.

From the impressive Chinggis Khaan Statue to the distinctive silhouette of Turtle Rock in Gorkhi-Terelj National Park, discover places that showcase the character and beauty of Mongolia.
