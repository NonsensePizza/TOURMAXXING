01 — THE EXPERIENCE

Find your escape in the wild north.

Northern Mongolia is a land of deep-blue lakes, forest-covered mountains, green valleys, and vast stretches of wilderness. Its landscapes offer a striking contrast to the desert scenery of the south.

From the remarkable shores of Lake Khuvsgul to the remote forests and traditional communities of the northern taiga, this region invites travelers to slow down, reconnect with nature, and discover another side of Mongolia.
