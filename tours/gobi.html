THE GOBI DESERT

A vast landscape full of wonder.

Stretching across southern Mongolia, the Gobi is a remarkable region of open plains, rocky terrain, rugged mountains, and sweeping desert scenery. Its landscapes change dramatically from one place to another.

A journey through the Gobi offers the chance to experience the quiet beauty of the countryside, learn about nomadic traditions, and see a side of Mongolia that feels a world away from busy city life.
